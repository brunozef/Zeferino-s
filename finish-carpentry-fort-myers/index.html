Finish carpentry

What we install

Zeferino's Precision Finish installs the interior finish work that makes a house look complete: crown molding, ceiling trim, baseboards, door and window casing, interior doors, stairs and handrails. We work on new construction and on remodels in Fort Myers.

Our Fort Myers work includes crown molding, tray ceiling trim and layered frame ceilings installed in new construction homes at Esplanade Lake Club, from foyers and hallways to great rooms and bedrooms.

For builders and general contractors we provide labor-only finish carpentry: you supply the materials and the schedule, we install. Homeowners get the same crew and the same attention to joints, alignment and clean transitions.

Included

  • Crown molding and tray ceiling trim
  • Layered frame and coffered-style ceiling trim
  • Baseboards and shoe molding
  • Door and window casing
  • Interior doors and door trim
  • Stair treads, risers and handrails

For homeowners, builders and general contractors. Labor-only installation is available.
