Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains how Zeferino's Precision Finish (Zeferino's Inc., finish carpentry) and Eva's Expert Cleaning Services LLC (residential and commercial cleaning) collect and use personal information through the website zeferinosusa.com, our estimate request forms and our Facebook and Instagram lead forms. Both businesses are located in Southwest Florida, USA.

Information we collect

When you request an estimate on our website or through a Facebook or Instagram lead form, we collect the information you choose to provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, city or ZIP code, the service you are interested in and any details you write about your project or home.

Our website also uses the Meta Pixel and standard server logs to understand visits to our pages (for example, which pages were viewed and whether a form was submitted) so we can measure and improve our advertising. These tools may use cookies.

How we use your information

  • To contact you about your estimate request and schedule services.
  • To prepare quotes, service agreements and invoices for the work you hire us to do.
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising.

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with service providers that help us run our business (such as website hosting, our customer database and email), and only for the purposes above, or when required by law.

Facebook and Instagram lead forms

If you submit a lead form in one of our Facebook or Instagram ads, Meta shares the information you entered with us. We receive it through our own app ("Eva Leads") using Meta's official Lead Ads tools, and we store it in our private customer database only to respond to your request.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while it is needed to provide services and keep business records, and then delete it.

Your choices and data deletion

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the personal information we have about you, including information received from Facebook or Instagram lead forms. Send your request to evasexpertcleaningservicesllc@gmail.com or zeferinosprecisionfinish@gmail.com with the name, phone number or email you used. We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings and manage ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including password-protected access and encrypted connections (HTTPS).
